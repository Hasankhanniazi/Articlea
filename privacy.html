Privacy Statement

This statement explains what information may be processed when you visit Articlea.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Information Articlea receives

Articlea does not require an account, ask visitors to submit personal profiles, or provide a comment or user-upload feature. The site does not intentionally collect personal information through forms.

Like most websites, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and systems involved in delivering the site may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security logs. That processing is governed by the relevant provider's policies and practices.

Cookies and local storage

Articlea's current site features do not require account cookies or browser local storage. However, hosting infrastructure or third-party services, if added in the future, may use technologies of their own. Your browser settings let you manage or block cookies.

External links and publishers

Articlea links to independent publisher websites. When you follow an external link, that website's privacy policy and terms apply. We do not control how third-party publishers collect or use information.

Children

Articlea is a general-audience discovery site and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and questions

This statement may be updated as the site changes. The latest version will be published on this page. If you have a privacy concern, use the project contact route available through the Articlea website or its public project repository; do not post sensitive personal information publicly.

Articlea is an independent discovery website. Publisher content remains with its original rights holders. For the complete story, follow the link to the original publisher.
