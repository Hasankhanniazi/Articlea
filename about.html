About Us

Articlea is an independent article-discovery website designed to help readers find interesting stories from publishers around the web.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Our purpose

We bring together headlines, publication details and short previews from selected public publisher feeds, so readers can discover stories across topics in one place.

Our current areas include World News, Sports, Anime & Comics, Entertainment, Technology and Gaming. Trending topics are detected automatically from the recent stories available in our feeds. They reflect activity in Articlea's collected sources, not a measure of worldwide popularity.

How Articlea works

Articlea collects publicly available RSS/feed information from publishers. We display headlines, available images, publisher names, dates and short feed excerpts, then link readers to the original publisher for the full story.

We do not intend to republish full articles, copy entire publisher websites, bypass paywalls or claim ownership of third-party reporting, photographs, logos or other materials.

Editorial independence

Articlea is a discovery tool, not the publisher of the stories it lists. Inclusion in a feed is not an endorsement, and headlines or excerpts may be supplied by third parties. Please visit the original publisher for complete context and corrections.

Articlea is an independent discovery website. Publisher content remains with its original rights holders. For the complete story, follow the link to the original publisher.
