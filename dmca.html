DMCA

Articlea respects intellectual-property rights and expects users and publishers to do the same.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Third-party material

Articlea is designed to display limited publisher feed information—such as headlines, short excerpts, attribution, dates and feed-provided images—and to direct readers to the original publisher. We do not intend to host full publisher articles or claim ownership of third-party content.

Submit a copyright notice

If you believe material displayed on Articlea infringes a copyright you own or are authorized to represent, please provide a written notice containing:

  • Your physical or electronic signature.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • The exact Articlea page URL and a clear description of the material you want reviewed.
  • Your name and contact information, including an email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury where applicable, that the information is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act for the rights holder.

Send the notice through the project contact route made available by the Articlea operator. Please do not publish personal contact details or legal documents in a public issue or comment. Articlea currently does not publish a dedicated DMCA-agent email address; one should be added here if a formal designated agent is appointed.

Review and response

We will review sufficiently detailed requests and may remove or disable access to the identified material while a claim is assessed. We may ask for additional information where necessary. Submitting a notice does not guarantee removal, and knowingly submitting materially false claims may have legal consequences.

Counter-notification

If material is removed following a copyright complaint and you believe the removal resulted from a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification through the same private project contact route. Include the material and its former location, your contact details, a statement under penalty of perjury that removal was due to mistake or misidentification, and your consent to the applicable legal process. Do not post this information publicly.

Important

This page provides general information and is not legal advice. If you are unsure about your rights or obligations, consult a qualified legal professional.
